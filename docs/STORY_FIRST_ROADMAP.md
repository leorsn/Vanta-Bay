# VANTA BAY — Story-First Production Roadmap

## Production rule
Story Mode is built and stabilized before Online Mode development begins.

Online networking, crews, matchmaking, server economy, PvP and multiplayer synchronization are deferred until the offline Story foundation is playable and structurally sound.

## Stage A — Core offline foundation
- Third-person character controller and camera
- Vehicles, entry/exit and driving
- World/district foundation
- Pedestrians and traffic
- Crime, witnesses, wanted and police
- Combat foundation
- Smartphone / VANTA OS
- Navigation and mission framework
- Cash, bank, REP and daily statement
- Local save slots and autosave
- Day/night and weather

## Stage B — Story Vertical Slice
Build BLACK GLASS as the first complete Story mission:
1. Jace starts in his apartment
2. Mateo sends a message
3. Player reads it in VANTA OS
4. Navigation points to the target garage
5. Player reaches and steals the vehicle
6. Witness reports the theft
7. Police pursuit begins
8. Player breaks line of sight
9. SEARCHING state
10. ESCAPED state
11. Deliver vehicle to Port Vanta workshop
12. Receive Cash/Bank and REP
13. Discover the encrypted storage-device story beat
14. Autosave Story progress

## Stage C — Story foundation expansion
Implement the first ten Story missions as the hidden tutorial arc:
- First Run
- No Questions
- After Midnight
- Wrong Place
- Black Glass
- Lose Them
- Clean Slate
- The Introduction
- Terms & Conditions
- Overhead

Systems introduced through missions rather than a separate tutorial.

## Stage D — Story world
Expand districts, interiors, contacts, businesses, properties, combat, boats, heists, relationships and story decisions. Maintain fully offline Story saves.

## Stage E — Story content
Build the wider Jace/Adrian campaign, side stories, major heists and post-story free roam.

## Stage F — Online foundation (DEFERRED)
Only after Story Mode foundation is proven:
- server-authoritative profile
- session/lobby system
- up to ~10 players
- networked vehicles/players
- relevant NPC synchronization
- crews/social systems
- online economy validation
- PvP/hostility
- co-op operations

## Architectural rule
Story Save and Online Profile must remain separate. Story must never require a network connection.
