# VANTA BAY — Story Missions 01–10

## 01 — FIRST RUN
Jace starts in his apartment with $1,500, a basic car and no weapon.
Gameplay: leave apartment, enter personal car, drive to Mateo's garage, deliver a small package.
Systems taught: movement, phone, navigation, entering vehicles, driving.

## 02 — NO QUESTIONS
Mateo asks Jace to move a vehicle across Old Bay.
Gameplay: collect vehicle, avoid a police checkpoint, deliver without damaging it heavily.
Systems taught: vehicle condition, alternate routes, light police presence.

## 03 — AFTER MIDNIGHT
Night delivery through Vanta Beach.
Gameplay: drive after dark, meet a contact, escape an attempted robbery.
Systems taught: day/night, basic melee/combat, health.

## 04 — WRONG PLACE
Jace witnesses a deal collapse.
Gameplay: reach an alley, survive the encounter, evade responding police, get back to apartment.
Systems taught: firearms foundation, witnesses, wanted/search/escape.

## 05 — BLACK GLASS
Steal a high-value Vellaro from an Ocean Drive private garage.
Gameplay: Mateo message, navigation, theft, witness report, 3-star pursuit, search/escape, Port Vanta delivery, payout, encrypted storage-device discovery.
Story: starts The Network thread.

## 06 — LOSE THEM
Jace realizes the storage device has attracted attention.
Gameplay: return to apartment, receive Mateo warning, relocate and avoid surveillance.
Systems taught: persistent Story state and safe-location flow.

## 07 — CLEAN SLATE
Remove the stolen vehicle trail and dispose of evidence.
Gameplay: workshop interaction, repaint/change plates prototype, deliver evidence to a disposal point.
Systems taught: vehicle identity and workshop services.

## 08 — THE INTRODUCTION
Jace meets a higher-level fixer.
Gameplay: travel to Marina District, social meeting, dialogue choices, drive a contact to a second location.
Systems taught: contacts and relationship state.

## 09 — TERMS & CONDITIONS
First structured criminal contract.
Gameplay: choose one of two approaches, acquire an item, optional stealth, payout variation.
Systems taught: mission approach choice and performance reward.

## 10 — OVERHEAD
First Adrian Vale appearance.
Gameplay: Jace observes a transaction connected to Adrian from a distance, then escapes a coordinated tail.
Story: transitions from street crime into the money/influence layer.

## Prototype implementation order
BLACK GLASS → LOSE THEM → FIRST RUN → NO QUESTIONS → WRONG PLACE → AFTER MIDNIGHT → CLEAN SLATE → THE INTRODUCTION → TERMS & CONDITIONS → OVERHEAD.

The unusual implementation order is intentional: BLACK GLASS is the Vertical Slice used to validate the core systems before backfilling the opening tutorial arc.
